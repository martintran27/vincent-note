Bài 1 (ngắn, ~1–2 phút)
ABC Company applies the perpetual inventory system and the gross method of accounting for sales. The following transactions during December:
December 1. Purchased $20,000 of merchandise from the Graint Store on cash.
December 10. Sold merchandise (purchased on December 1) to Osimi Company for $28,000 on credit.
December 12. Purchased $4,000 of office supplies on cash.
December 15. Received a full payment from Osimi Company.
Request: Prepare journal entries to record the transactions.
Bài 2 (dài, có điều chỉnh và khoá sổ, ~3 phút)
ABC Company applies the perpetual inventory system and the gross method of accounting for sales. The following transactions during December:
December 4: Purchased $12,000 of office equipment for cash.
December 8: Provided services to Blue Tech for $15,000 on credit.
December 16: Paid $2,000 in cash for rent for the office space for the month of December.
December 21: Received a full payment from Blue Tech.
Request:
(1) Prepare journal entries to record the transactions.
(2) Prepare the adjusted entries on December 31:
(a) A physical count of supplies at December 31 shows $1,800 of supplies available. The Supplies account had a debit balance of $2,500 on December 1.
(b) The company received $36,000 cash in advance for training services. As of December 31, half of the training services had been performed.
(3) Prepare the closing entries necessary on December 31.
Bài 3 (tính giá vốn, ~2 phút)
A retail store starts January with 120 units of inventory, each costing $5. On January 8, the store purchases an additional 80 units at $6 each. On January 12, the store sells 150 units. On January 20, it buys 100 more units at $7 each. On January 25, the store sells 50 units.
Using FIFO, calculate the cost of goods sold (COGS) for the sales on January 12 and January 25.
